01 / ABOUT METHE PERSON BEHIND THE RESEARCH

SCIENCE WITH PURPOSE

Sustainability isn't a slogan.
It's a system to improve.

I work at the intersection of life cycle assessment, circular materials, engineering research and computational analysis.

My path spans industrial operations at AkzoNobel, sustainable materials research at CERMAV–CNRS, and comparative environmental assessment at NOVA FCT. I am currently pursuing the EIT RawMaterials AMIR master’s across Portugal and Spain, with a focus on urban mining, recycling and construction materials.

I am particularly interested in how AI-assisted analysis and transparent LCA can support resource recovery and lower-impact material choices. My early-stage innovation concept, Terralynq, explores that intersection; I distinguish planned capabilities from validated results.
